Project Proposal & Documentation

Eunoiaverse Multilingual Regional Dictionary is a community-driven initiative aimed at safeguarding, preserving, and making indigenous languages in East Kalimantan (such as Tunjung, Benuaq, Kenyah, Bahau, and Kutai) digitally accessible.

Key Objectives:
  • Open-source collection of local vocabulary.
  • Real-time browser-based translation tools.
  • Collaborative contributions directly from speakers.
